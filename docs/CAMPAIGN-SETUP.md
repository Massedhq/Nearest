# Student launch campaign — before you open it

The campaign stays in **Draft** (students can't see or join it) until these four things are done in
**Admin → People → Launch campaign → Settings**. The dashboard lists anything still missing.

1. **Profile picture graphic** — tap "Use the supplied Nearest graphic" (logo + usenearest.com, sized for circle crops) or upload your own.
2. **How to participate** — the seven steps are filled in. Keep the incentive disclosure line.
3. **Campaign terms** — a draft is filled in. Have it reviewed, tick "I've reviewed these terms", and **Publish**.
4. **Booking opens** — preset to November 19, 2026 at 12:00 AM Central. Change the time if needed.

Optional: **What the free service includes** — leave blank to use the standard wording.

Then go to the campaign dashboard and tap **Go live**. The big status at the top shows DRAFT, LIVE, PAUSED or CLOSED.
Use **Pause** to stop new students joining (students already in keep going) and **Stop (close enrollment)** to end enrollment.

Professionals (nothing for you to add or pay)
- New pros tick the launch campaign box on their last setup page (under the $5 invite reward box), choose one service listed at $25 or more, and set how many free appointments they give (5 to start).
- Pros who joined earlier get a home-screen card: **Opt in** or **Decline**. No emails are sent.
- The pro receives no payout for a free appointment, and accepting one never starts their membership.
- See who agreed, declined or hasn't answered on the dashboard; Settings → Participating professionals lists each one's service and free appointments, with Pause / Resume.

Notes
- Capacity defaults to 750 places total across DFW. Market defaults to DFW (students' school market).
- For the strongest privacy, use a **private** Vercel Blob store. If the store is public, screenshots are saved as unlisted files and are still only ever shown through Nearest's signed-in screenshot link.
- The seven-day clock is elapsed-time tracking plus manual evidence review. It doesn't monitor social media or prove the picture stayed up the whole time.
