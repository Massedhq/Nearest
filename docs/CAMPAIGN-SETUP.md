# Student launch campaign — before you open it

The campaign stays in **Draft** (students can't see or join it) until every item below is done in
**Admin → People → Launch campaign → Settings & rewards**. The dashboard lists anything still missing.

1. **Profile picture graphic** — tap "Use the supplied Nearest graphic" (logo + usenearest.com, sized for circle crops) or upload your own.
2. **Student instructions** — a starter version is filled in. Keep the incentive disclosure line.
3. **Campaign terms** — a draft is filled in. Have it reviewed, tick "I've reviewed these terms", and **Publish**.
4. **Booking opens** — preset to November 19, 2026 at 12:00 AM Central. Change the time if needed.
5. **What the free service includes** — a title and exact description. Students see this before joining, so only describe what's arranged in step 6.
6. **Reward arrangements** — for each professional who has agreed: choose them, their service, what Nearest pays them per appointment, how many appointments they agreed to, and a note of how/when they agreed. Students pay $0 (no deposit, service fee or travel fee; at the professional's location).
7. **Confirm funding** — tick the confirmation that Nearest has arranged and will fund these payouts.

Then go to the campaign dashboard and tap **Open the campaign**.

Notes
- Capacity defaults to 750 places total across DFW. Market defaults to DFW (students' school market).
- For the strongest privacy, use a **private** Vercel Blob store. If the store is public, screenshots are saved as unlisted files and are still only ever shown through Nearest's signed-in screenshot link.
- The seven-day clock is elapsed-time tracking plus manual evidence review. It doesn't monitor social media or prove the picture stayed up the whole time.
